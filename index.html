a random summer day

It was just a random day.

You were a stranger, and so was I.

Neither of us knew what was about to happen.

We started talking, we bloop-blooped, it was summer, and it was hot outside.

🫠🫠

🫠🫠

I still remember the little things, the "Guess who?" followed by "It's meee".

Eventually, we left that app behind, but somehow, we never really left each other.

I started liking you more than I probably should have.

You told me how things were, that there were circumstances, boundaries, and that we could never be more than friends.

And I understood. Or at least, I tried to.

I kept my feelings quiet because the last thing I ever wanted was to put you in a difficult position or make things harder for you.

I thought that, eventually, our conversations would become less frequent, that we'd slowly drift apart until we became nothing more than a memory.

But we didn't.

We kept talking. And talking.

And somehow, every conversation made me want another one.

Every day made you a little more important to me.

Until one day, I gathered enough courage to tell you what had been growing inside me for so long.

You understood, but you told me that it was unlikely you'd ever feel the same way.

I didn't know what to do with that feeling, but I stayed.

Not because I expected anything in return, but because having you in my life, even just as a friend, meant more to me than I could explain.

And then, with time, something changed.

Your feelings changed. Mine never really did.

I told you I loved you, and it took you a little while, but eventually, you said those words too.

I love you too.

I don't think you understand how much those four words meant to me.

September

Then September came.

I remember how difficult it was.

I felt like I was burning alive, and I know you weren't doing much better either.

Somehow, we made it through.

Christmas

Then Christmas came around, and it was almost the same story.

I genuinely thought I'd be able to handle it a little better the second time, that maybe it wouldn't hurt as much.

But it did.

And somewhere along the way, without even realizing when it happened, you became one of the most central parts of my life.

You became someone I look forward to, someone I miss, someone whose presence can change the entire feeling of my day.

And the strangest, most beautiful part is that I still love you more with every passing day.

More than I did when I first realized I had fallen for you.

More than I thought I could ever love someone.

We've had our fights. Little ones, big ones, and some that felt enormous.

There were moments when things could have ended, moments when you could have chosen your peace of mind and walked away.

I wouldn't have blamed you.

I know I haven't always been right.

I've made mistakes, I've hurt you, and there were times when you had every reason to give up on me.

But you stayed.

You gave me chances. You listened.

You forgave me, even when I wasn't sure I deserved it.

And I don't ever want you to think that I take any of that for granted.

I'm thankful for you.

For every conversation, every laugh, every silly little moment, every difficult day we somehow got through, and even for the moments that taught me how much you mean to me.

Sometimes I think about how absurdly random it all was.

Two strangers, on an ordinary day, on an ordinary app, with absolutely no idea that they'd eventually become this important to each other.

If I could go back to that random summer day, knowing everything I know now, I'd still start that conversation.

I'd still say hello. I'd still choose to get to know you.

Even knowing about September, Christmas, the difficult days, the tears, and the fights.

Because I'd know that somewhere along the way, I'd find you.

And if there's one thing I want you to take from all of this, it's that I hope you never feel like you have to become someone else for me.

Never change just to make yourself easier to love.

I love you as you are, with all the little things that make you you.

Thank you for staying.

Thank you for loving me.

Thank you for being part of my life.

And if I could ask for just one thing, it would be that we keep finding our way back to each other, no matter how difficult some days get.

I love you.

I loved you then, I love you now, and I hope I get to keep loving you for a very, very long time.
